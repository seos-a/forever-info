Methods and Caveats

Where the numbers come fromThe census on Life on Azeroth, which finds characters on guild rosters. Players who aren't in a guild aren't counted, so this shows who plays in guilds, not the whole server population.

FiltersThe crests at the top show one faction's races only, and every share is then of that faction. The flags count one region's realms only (Americas, Europe, Korea, Taiwan), and the ruleset icons one kind of realm (Normal, PvP, Roleplay, Roleplay PvP); pick a crest, flag or ruleset again to go back to all.

Snapshot Blank cells in the table are race and class combinations WoW Forever doesn't allow.
