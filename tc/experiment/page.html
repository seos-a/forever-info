Methods and Caveats

  • WoWSims Forever at commit 5d4dd1c (27 October 2026, 12:01 PM CT), whose engine has the 26 October racials and the 27 October changes to Blood Fury, Berserking and Touch of the Grave, run by Claude from the sims' own source.
  • Every DPS spec's preset builds from the WoWSims site: gear, talents, rotation, consumables, buffs and debuffs as each spec's presets set them, except Dragonbreath Chili: only the Retribution and Enhancement presets carry it, and it would put them about 3% (1 target) to 11% (5 targets) ahead of the other melee specs, so it is left out. WoWSims' Rogue presets set no professions, so the Rogue builds are given Engineering and Leatherworking, with the Warriors' Goblin Sapper Charge and EZ-Thro Dark Bomb, like the other melee builds. The gear presets are marked developer-only, so the public site hides them; they are in WoWSims' source.
  • Fights of 1, 2, 3 and 5 minutes against the site's default level 63 boss, 10,000 iterations each, with every race on the same random seed, so each DPS figure is good to about 0.1%.
  • Gain over no racials compares against the same build with no race: no racial abilities or passives and no race stat offset. Stat offsets count, which is why Tauren and some Dwarf builds come out below zero.
  • Targets (DPS Ranking by Spec): each build at 1 to 5 copies of the boss, run with the race that does best on it at one target. Race choice barely changes with the number of targets, so only that race is run there. The target switches on the DPS by Race and Spec table and the Racial Bonus box are the exception: every race, and the no-racials baseline, is run at 1 to 5 targets for them.
  • Active racials such as Elune's Light, Blood Fury, Berserking and Eureka! are fired by the sim's rotation on its own. Players lining them up with other cooldowns may get a little more.
  • Situational racials are not counted: Big Game Hunter, Beast Slaying, Elemental Insight, and Read Leyline outside the mage and feral rotations.

Things to keep in mind

  • Gear is not re-optimized per race. Most physical presets are already hit capped, so Dwarf's 1% hit (Artisan's Precision) adds nothing there and its lower Agility shows as a loss. A separate run gave Dwarf gear with 1% less hit and 1% more crit (spell hit and spell crit for casters), as a Dwarf would gear. That adds about 1 point on Hunters and Arms, 0.7 on Fury, 0.5 on Enhancement, 0.3 on Ret, and almost nothing on Rogues (dual-wield white hits still miss well past the 8% cap) or Shadow (7% hit against a 17% spell miss chance); on Elemental it's a loss, so a Dwarf would keep the hit. It lifts Dwarf from 9th to 4th of 10 on Arms and from 4th to 2nd on Enhancement, but it isn't the top race on any spec. The tables show the presets as they are.
  • Classic pre-raid gear (the Gear switch): the same builds, talents, rotations, consumables and buffs, in each spec's Phase 1 pre-raid best-in-slot list. Most of those items are not in Forever's item database, so they are added to the sim with their Classic stats (10 hit rating per 1% hit, 14 crit rating per 1% crit); where Forever has its own version of an item, that version is used. Hand of Justice is left out: Hunters wear Royal Seal of Eldre'Thalas in its place, everyone else Rune of the Guard Captain. Item swaps are off, both Moonkin presets become one build, Frost Mage wears the mage list with Frozen Wrath suffixes, and effects the sim doesn't model (Second Wind's mana, the Dwarven Hand Cannon's cannonball) are left out.
  • Dwarf in Classic pre-raid gear. Artisan's Precision gives Dwarf 1% hit, so a Dwarf can leave one 1%-hit piece of the list behind for a piece without hit from another Classic pre-raid list (same slot, one it can wear) and still have the hit every other race has. The sim tried every such swap and kept the best one only where it beat the plain list: Blackstone Ring for Magni's Will (Arms, ranged Survival) or Maiden's Circle (Enhancement), and Slashclaw Bracers for Deepfury Bracers (Marksmanship and Survival weave). Every other build wears the plain list as Dwarf too.
  • Placeholder presets. Elemental Shaman, Enhancement Shaman and Shadow Priest run WoWSims' developer test presets (named "Testing" there, named after the spec here), so treat those specs with more caution.
  • Specs. Each build counts toward its talent tree: Frostfire Bolt (29 Fire points) as Fire, SM/Ruin (31 Affliction points) as Affliction. WoWSims' Smite Priest build is left out, as it isn't a competitive DPS spec; Ret Paladin's Holy Shock and Twist of Light builds are both Retribution.
  • WoWSims' model of the Forever beta changes daily, and the gaps between the top races are often under 1%.

Methods and Caveats

The sim. ForeverHealing-TC's experimental raid sim (game build 1.60.1.70425, data of 16 October 2026), with each healer's default level 60 setup, gear and priority list from the tool. Results for Holy Priest, Restoration Druid and Restoration Shaman. The default raid: 2 tanks, 5 healers (2 Restoration Shamans on the tanks, Priest, Paladin and Druid on the raid), 6 melee and 7 ranged, steady tank damage and a raid-wide hit every 20 sec.

Racials patched to the 26 and 27 October notes (the sim still has the 15 October versions):

  • Human: Ruthless 1% crit (was 2%); The Human Spirit 10% Spirit (was 5%).
  • Dwarf: Earth Fury (2% stronger crits) replaced by Artisan's Precision (1% hit), which does nothing for heals.
  • Tauren: Endurance no longer makes crits stronger.
  • Orc: Savage (1% crit) replaced by Command (pet damage), which does nothing for heals.
  • Troll: Berserking lasts 15 sec (was 10; 27 October).
  • Orc: Blood Fury on a 2 min cooldown (was 3; 27 October).
  • Skyborne: Wind Blessed 2% haste (was 3%). The sim did not apply this haste at all, so it is now applied to cast times through the sim's own cast speed hook.

Haste doesn't show up in this sim. The sim moves in 0.05 sec steps, so a cast made 2% faster (2.50 to 2.45 sec) still ends on the same step, and Skyborne and Troll come out exactly level with no racials. A check at 0.01 sec steps didn't change the picture: the healers' priority lists pace their casts to their mana, so the Shamans cast the same number of spells either way. Read Skyborne's and Troll's results as "haste not measured", not as "haste is worthless".

Racial cooldowns are averaged over the fight, assuming each is used on cooldown from the pull: Elune's Light (+10% crit, 15 sec, 3 min), Berserking (+10% cast speed, 15 sec, 3 min), Eureka! (+10% healing, 8 sec, 2 min) and Blood Fury (+10% healing power, 15 sec, 2 min). On a 2 minute fight Elune's Light counts as +1.25% crit. The sim can't time them, so lining them up with heavy damage would be worth more than shown. Eureka!'s cost reduction isn't known yet and is left out.

A healing-limited fight. In the default fight the healers keep up with the damage, so a better race mostly overheals and the five healers compete for the same heals. That made race differences unmeasurable (about ±2% noise against 0.5–1% effects). So raid health is raised 100× and damage 2.5×: there is always more to heal, nobody dies, overhealing nearly vanishes, and each healer's effective healing is what its spells and mana allow. Real fights are rarely like this: there, crit and haste racials are worth less (more of the extra healing is overheal) and mana racials matter only when mana runs out.

Holy Paladin is left out. Its priority list waits for someone to drop below a share of their health before casting, and with 100× health that almost never happens: it healed about 5,000 in 2 minutes (about 46,000 in the sim's normal fight) and sat idle three quarters of the time, so its race results were meaningless. The other healers also wait for low health on some rules and idle more than in the normal fight (Shamans about 45% of the time instead of under 10%), so heal size and mana count for more here than speed.

Runs. 40 runs per race, healer and fight length, every race on the same random seeds, at 2, 3, 5 and 7 minutes. Only the measured healer's race changes; the other healers keep their default races (Undead Priest and Paladin, Night Elf Druid, Dwarf Shamans). Both Shamans take the race being measured and their results are averaged.

Encounter types. Unlike the rest of this page, the Healers by Encounter Type box runs at the sim's normal health and damage, since the question there is how each healer copes with each kind of fight, not how races compare. Each healer has no racials, 40 runs per damage preset (the presets' level 60 values), 3 minute fights. The raid is the sim's default one, so the Shamans heal the tanks and the Priest and Druid heal the raid; that assignment, not only the healer, shapes each situation's numbers. With 100× health these presets didn't work: in the lighter ones nobody dropped low enough for the healers' rules to fire, and the Shamans sat idle over 95% of the fight.

Not counted:

  • Read Leyline (Alliance Skyborne): +50% regen for 15 sec, or Attuned, +100% for an hour, after using it at a ley line. Not in the sim. If ley lines are near raids, Attuned would be a large mana gain for Alliance Skyborne Druids and Shamans.
  • Undead Cannibalize (35% of maximum mana from a corpse). The sim only uses it on its mana page.
  • With 100× health nobody drops below 35%, so emergency rules never fire: emergency Flash Heals, shields, and the Dwarf Priest's Desperate Prayer. The Undead Priest's Dark Sacrifice (health turned into mana) was never cast either, so Undead comes out level with no racials.
  • Race base stats: the sim gives every race the same base Intellect and Spirit, so only racial traits differ.

Methods and Caveats

  • WoWSims Forever at commit 5d4dd1c (27 October 2026, 12:01 PM CT), whose engine has the 26 October racials and the 27 October changes to Blood Fury, Berserking and Touch of the Grave. Run by Claude from the sims' own source with each tank spec's test-suite setup: talents, rotation, consumables and buffs, and the UI's gear presets. Protection Warrior has two (Defense Cap and Threat); Protection Paladin and Feral Bear have one each.
  • The default level 63 boss hits the tank every 2 sec. Incoming healing is each spec's UI default (815 healing per second for Protection Warrior, 2,200 for Protection Paladin and Feral Bear), so no tank died in any run.
  • Dwarf tanks press Stoneform (10% less physical damage taken for 8 sec, 3 min cooldown) whenever it is ready. WoWSims' Prot Warrior rotation keeps it for below 50% health, which the steady healing never reaches, so it would never be used. The Prot Paladin's sim fires it at the pull together with Templar's Bulwark, whose 8 sec absorb shield soaks every hit while both last, so the Paladin presses it once the Bulwark drops.
  • Fights of 1, 2, 3 and 5 minutes, 10,000 iterations each, with every race on the same random seed. Each figure is good to about 0.1–0.5%; Tie marks races within two standard errors of the best.
  • Targets (Tank Ranking by Spec): each build at 1 to 5 targets, run with the race that generates the most threat on it at one target. Only the first target attacks; the others take hits and add threat work but deal no damage, so damage taken and spikes stay one-target figures. The Racial Bonus box's target switch runs every race, and the no-racials baseline, at 1 to 5 targets; it applies to threat and DPS only.
  • Gain over no racials compares against the same setup with no race: no racial abilities or passives and no race stat offset.
  • Threat (TPS) is the threat generated per second. Damage taken (DTPS) is after armor, avoidance and mitigation. Spikes (TMI) is the Theck–Meloree Index, a measure of how large the tank's worst damage spikes are against its health. For damage taken and spikes, lower is better, so the page treats the lowest value as 100%.

Things to keep in mind

  • Gear is not re-optimized per race. A Dwarf or Night Elf tank would trade some gear for other stats in practice.
  • Stoneform, Elune's Light, Blood Fury and Berserking are fired by the sim's rotation on its own; a player could time defensive ones for big hits.
  • Situational racials are not counted (Big Game Hunter, Beast Slaying, Elemental Insight), and the boss is a Mechanical mob.
