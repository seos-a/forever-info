Methods and Caveats

Where the numbers come fromThe Raider.IO WoW Forever census. Characters below level 10 aren't included. Raider.IO only knows the characters it has come across, mostly through guild rosters: characters outside a guild, or ones it hasn't looked up, can be missing.

Weekly changeGreen and red arrows show how much a class's or race's share has changed in the past 7 days. Moves under 0.2 points aren't shown, nor any in a selection of fewer than 1,000 characters, as small samples can swing a lot from week to week.

UpdatesBy hand about once a day, not live (Raider.IO updates its census every hour). The date in the bottom-left corner is when this copy was taken.

NotesCounts in-game characters, not real-life players.
